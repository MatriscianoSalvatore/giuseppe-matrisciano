About me.

Italian marketing graduate student at the University of Miami, passionate about digital storytelling, consumer insights, and connecting brands with people.

I'm from Bologna, Italy, and I moved to the United States five years ago to continue my education. Moving to a new country taught me how to adapt to new situations, work hard, and stay open to learning from different people.

At the University of Georgia I studied Consumer Economics and developed skills in data analysis, problem-solving, marketing and consumer behavior, alongside internships in banking and public administration. Now I'm building the creative side on top of that analytical base.

What makes me different is my international background. I've lived and studied in both Italy and the United States, and it has made me a strong communicator, a quick learner, and someone who enjoys working with people from different backgrounds.

Raised under the porticoes. Up at 3 a.m. for the bakery.

I was born and raised in Bologna, in northern Italy. I think it's a very underrated city. At 19 I didn't get into the graphic and interior design program I had applied for, so I went to work with my dad at his bakery: awake at three every morning to make bread and pizza. It taught me a lot, including that it wasn't what I wanted to do for the rest of my life.

In 2020 I spent six months as a full-time intern at Emil Banca. Then COVID kept Italy at home, and gave me time to think. I needed a big change: going back to school, this time in the United States.

Point of view

AI will transform marketing. It won't replace marketers.

A thread that started early: in November 2021, three months after landing in Florida, my class presentation in the Intensive English Program was on artificial intelligence in cars.

i.

Speed

AI can analyze large amounts of data much faster than people can. Companies spot trends sooner and respond more quickly to changes in consumer behavior.

ii.

Personalization

Instead of the same campaign for everyone, brands can understand each customer's interests and deliver more relevant content and better experiences.

iii.

People

Marketing is not just about data. Creativity, communication and understanding human emotions are still what build strong relationships with customers.

The best results will come from combining AI with human creativity.
