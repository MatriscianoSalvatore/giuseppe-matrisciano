AI will transform marketing. It won't replace marketers.

Three months after I landed in Florida, my first class presentation in English was about artificial intelligence in cars. Five years later, the same question keeps coming back in my marketing classes: what does AI change, and what does it leave to people?

What AI is good at: speed

AI can analyze large amounts of data much faster than people can. Companies spot trends sooner and respond more quickly to changes in consumer behavior.

What it makes possible: personalization

Instead of running the same campaign for everyone, brands can understand each customer's interests and deliver more relevant content and better experiences.

What it can't do: the human part

Marketing is not just about data. Creativity, communication and understanding human emotions are still what build strong relationships with customers. AI is a very useful tool, but it cannot replace people.

The best results will come from combining AI with human creativity.

In five years I see myself working between creative and analytics teams, using consumer insights and data to make better marketing decisions. AI will be part of that work, not a replacement for it.
